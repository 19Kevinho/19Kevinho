
# Hi, I'm Kevin Vargas

I'm a Mechanical Engineer with international experience in **Research & Development (R&D), Mechanical Design, and Manufacturing** of advanced energy systems.

My expertise focuses on **CAD, CFD, FEA, and Design Optimization**, using tools such as SolidWorks, OpenFOAM, Altair EDEM, and R.

I'm a **Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional (CSWP)** with experience working with multidisciplinary engineering teams across the United States, Europe, and Latin America.

My work focuses on connecting engineering design, numerical simulation, and experimental methodologies to develop and optimize real-world engineering solutions.

## Areas of Expertise

- **Mechanical Design:** 3D CAD, assemblies, technical drawings, and prototyping.
- **CFD:** Fluid flow analysis and thermal systems.
- **FEA:** Structural analysis and design validation.
- **Particle Simulation:** Discrete Element Method (DEM).
- **Design Optimization:** Design of Experiments (DoE) and Response Surface Methodology (RSM).
- **Manufacturing:** Production processes, assembly, and engineering development.
